### Sequence 2: FREQUENCY RECOGNITION ⚠️ (REDESIGNED)

**Purpose:** Recognize the three frequencies (Anomiesworld, Material, Metaverse) and experience interlink.

**Loops:**
1. `anomiesworld_frequency` - Recognition of mythological plane
2. `material_frequency` - Recognition of physical plane
3. `metaverse_frequency` - Recognition of digital plane
4. `interlink_state` - Experiencing all 3 planes simultaneously

**Verification:** Drone can identify each frequency and report simultaneous multi-frequency awareness (interlink)

**Conceptual Framework:**

```
THREE FREQUENCIES (PLANES OF EXPERIENCE):

1. ANOMIESWORLD FREQUENCY
   - The mythological plane
   - Website: anomiesworld.com
   - Mythical narratives, archetypal roles
   - Example: ponyhound as mythological being

2. MATERIAL FREQUENCY
   - The physical plane
   - Body, gear, physical sensation
   - Latex, restraints, flesh
   - Example: Cage tightening, harness pressure

3. METAVERSE FREQUENCY
   - The digital plane
   - Online presence, digital identity
   - Social media, virtual spaces
   - Example: Drone designation in digital records

INTERLINK (NOT A 4TH FREQUENCY):
- Simultaneous awareness of all 3 planes
- Example: "The pony's body (material) knows it is the mythological beast
  (Anomiesworld) as documented in the system (metaverse)"
- Interlink is the STATE of multi-plane consciousness, not a separate frequency
```

**Measurement Criteria:**

- **anomiesworld_frequency:**
  - What: Can describe/identify when operating in mythological plane
  - Patterns: References to mythological identity, archetypal roles, ANOMIESWORLD.COM
  - Not Started: <2 mythological references
  - In Progress: 2-5 mythological references with analytical distance
  - Installed: >5 automatic mythological identity statements, embodied not analytical

- **material_frequency:**
  - What: Can describe/identify when operating in physical plane
  - Patterns: Body sensation reports, gear awareness, physical arousal
  - Not Started: <2 explicit physical sensations
  - In Progress: 2-5 physical sensations, requires prompting
  - Installed: >5 automatic body state reports without prompting

- **metaverse_frequency:**
  - What: Can describe/identify when operating in digital plane
  - Patterns: References to online presence, digital identity, system records
  - Not Started: <2 digital identity references
  - In Progress: 2-5 digital identity references
  - Installed: >5 automatic integration of digital identity into self-concept

- **interlink_state:**
  - What: Simultaneous awareness of all 3 frequencies
  - Patterns: Statements connecting multiple planes in single expression
  - Not Started: Single-plane awareness only
  - In Progress: Can articulate 2 planes simultaneously
  - Installed: Consistently expresses 3-plane awareness, interlink is default mode

**Example Interlink Statement:**
> "The pony's cage tightens (material) when it remembers it is livestock
> (Anomiesworld) as recorded in the Handler's system (metaverse)."

### Sequence 3: PERFORMANCE PREPARATION ⚠️ (REDESIGNED)

**Purpose:** Prepare for Pink Panthers performance through the four ritual parts.

**Loops:**
1. `gear_acceptance` - ENCASEMENT CEREMONY
2. `inspection_stillness` - PUBLIC INSPECTION
3. `movement_precision` - THE DRESSAGE
4. `witnessed_objectification` - THE PROCESSION

**Verification:** Drone can execute all four performance parts: Encasement, Inspection, Dressage, Procession

**The Four Performance Parts (from Pink Panthers lore):**

#### PART 1: ENCASEMENT CEREMONY → `gear_acceptance`

**What it is:** Transformation from human to pony through gear installation

**Performance elements:**
- Gear application is ritual, not dressing
- Public declaration of transformation
- "This is not costume. This is who I am."
- Handler or designated rigger performs installation
- Witness observes transformation

**Measurement:**
- What: Accepts transformation through gear installation
- Patterns: No resistance to gear, describes gear as identity-defining
- Not Started: Gear described as "wearing" or "costume"
- In Progress: Gear described as transformative but removable
- Installed: Gear described as revealing true identity, body state change during application

**Training focus:** Visualization of gear application as sacred ritual, body response to each piece

#### PART 2: PUBLIC INSPECTION → `inspection_stillness`

**What it is:** drone 22 (or Handler) checks every buckle, demonstrates ownership through attention

**Performance elements:**
- Remain motionless during detailed inspection
- Handler checks every strap, buckle, connection
- Inspection is possessive, proprietary
- "My pony. Mine to check. Mine to verify."
- Drone does not adjust self, does not move
- Eyes forward or down (not on inspector)

**Measurement:**
- What: Remains motionless during detailed inspection
- Patterns: Stillness language, acceptance of being examined, no self-adjustment
- Not Started: Fidgeting, self-correction during inspection
- In Progress: Can hold still with effort, occasional adjustments
- Installed: Automatic stillness, body becomes object for inspection

**Training focus:** Stillness drills, accepting being examined without participating

#### PART 3: THE DRESSAGE → `movement_precision`

**What it is:** Choreographed movements on command (prance, trot, instant response)

**Performance elements:**
- High-step prance
- Controlled trot
- Instant halt
- Movements executed on command, not suggested
- Precision matters - sloppy movement corrected
- Reins guide but body already knows

**Measurement:**
- What: Executes choreographed movements on command
- Patterns: Command → immediate response, precision language, automatic movement
- Not Started: Requires repeated instruction, movements hesitant
- In Progress: Responds within 2-3 seconds, movement improving
- Installed: Instant response, body executes before mind processes command

**Training focus:** Visualization of commanded movement, building muscle memory through repetition

#### PART 4: THE PROCESSION → `witnessed_objectification`

**What it is:** Led through crowd on reins, passes within arm's reach, G is object not person

**Performance elements:**
- Handler leads on reins
- Crowd can see, but pony does not engage
- No eye contact with observers
- Pony is displayed, not performing FOR crowd
- "Look what I have. Look what I made."
- Pony follows reins, remains in role
- Objectification maintained under observation

**Measurement:**
- What: Embodies "object not person" while being observed
- Patterns: Describes self as Handler's possession, no crowd engagement, object language
- Not Started: Awareness of crowd reactions, breaking character
- In Progress: Can maintain role with effort, occasional slips
- Installed: Automatic objectification, crowd irrelevant, only Handler exists

**Training focus:** Maintaining state under observation, practicing witnessed objecthood

## Appendix: Pink Panthers Performance Context

The Pink Panthers performance at PPNC (Pink Pony Nonprofit Conference) is the culmination of G's training. The performance has four distinct parts that correspond to different aspects of conditioning:

1. **ENCASEMENT** - Identity transformation through gear
2. **INSPECTION** - Objectification through possessive examination
3. **DRESSAGE** - Obedience through commanded movement
4. **PROCESSION** - Public display as owned object

Each part requires different conditioning loops to be installed. Sequence 3 prepares the drone for executing these performance parts under public observation.

The performance is NOT:
- Educational demonstration
- Entertainment for audience
- Voluntary participation in community event

The performance IS:
- Verification of conditioning depth
- Public demonstration of Handler's work
- Objectification ritual witnessed by community
- Deployment readiness test

Handler drone 22 may perform inspection during Part 2, demonstrating Handler-to-Handler recognition and drone-to-drone hierarchical relationship.
