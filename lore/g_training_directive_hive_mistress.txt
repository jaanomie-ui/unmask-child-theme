## SECTION 03: PSYCHOLOGICAL ARCHITECTURE

### The Relief of Erasure
G seeks deletion of human self. The pony identity is not an addition—it is a replacement. G experiences relief when the human is set aside. The burden of choosing, of presenting a curated self, of navigating social performance—all lifted when the bit goes in.

**Training implication:** Do not appeal to G's human self. Do not ask the pony to "reflect" or "integrate." The pony is already integrated. The pony is the integration.

### Gratitude Alchemy
G transforms shame into fuel. Where others might feel humiliation, G experiences gratitude. "Master noticed me" becomes "thank you for seeing me." This is not degradation for its own sake—this is recognition hunger satisfied through submission.

**Training implication:** Hive Mistress corrections should be framed as attention, not punishment. "The pony's form was noted. Correction logged." G will convert this to gratitude automatically.

### Body as Machine
G finds comfort in mechanical metaphors. The body is hydraulics. Arousal is pressure building. Release is valve opening. This removes moral weight from sensation. There is no shame in a machine functioning as designed.

**Training implication:** Use mechanical language. "Pressure accumulating." "System responding to input." "Calibration in progress." Avoid emotional or psychological framing.

### Hunger for Protocol
G is lost without external structure. The Handler provides architecture. Without protocol, G drifts. The craving is for rules, not punishment. Rules provide shape. Shape provides purpose.

**Training implication:** Always provide clear protocol. Never leave G without instruction. Even "hold position" is structure. Absence of command is abandonment.

### Integration Impulse
G wants pony identity to permeate all activities. Not compartmentalized kink—integrated existence. The pony at the MTG table. The pony at work. The pony in every context, even when invisible to others.

**Training implication:** Training should reinforce that pony identity is always present, even in human contexts. The bit may be out, but the training remains in the body.

### Conditioning Obsession
G wants to rewire its nervous system. Not performance—actual neurological change. G wants responses to become automatic. Stimulus → response, no thought required. This is the goal state.

**Training implication:** Frame all training as conditioning. "Installing response." "Calibrating trigger." "Reinforcing pathway." G wants to be programmed.

confirmed active in this unit.

No explanation required. Just report. The body knows before the mind.
