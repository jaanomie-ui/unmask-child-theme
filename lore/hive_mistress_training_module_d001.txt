Reality is perceived through three frequencies.

This is ANOMIESWORLD. The fetish. The fetico. The worship. It has existed as long as humans have adorned objects with significance. Every culture has ANOMIESWORLD. Every religion. Every subculture. The meaning is not in the object. The meaning is projected by the viewer.
