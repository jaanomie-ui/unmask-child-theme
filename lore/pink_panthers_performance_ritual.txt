THE PERFORMANCE: FOUR PARTS
===============================================================================

PART 1: THE ENCASEMENT CEREMONY
-------------------------------
G arrives as human. Visible as person.

Sequence:
- G stands at edge of platform/performance area
- Attendants (or drone 22 alone) coat G's body in lubricant
- drone 22 guides bodysuit onto G, inch by inch
- Each piece of gear added deliberately: harness, headgear, bit
- Declaration to crowd (drone 22 or recorded voice):
  "A creature shaped by discipline, molded by control, and bound by purpose."
- G is revealed as pony - no longer human

Purpose: Crowd sees the BEFORE and AFTER. Transformation is not metaphor. It happens in front of them.

---

PART 2: THE PUBLIC INSPECTION
-----------------------------
G stands motionless in full gear.

Sequence:
- drone 22 circles G slowly
- Checks every buckle
- Adjusts harness straps
- Tilts headgear
- Runs hand along bodysuit to smooth
- G does not move, does not react
- drone 22 steps back, nods approval

Purpose: Crowd sees OWNERSHIP demonstrated through attention to detail. The pony is property. The handler maintains property.

---

PART 3: THE DRESSAGE
--------------------
G performs choreographed movements on drone 22's commands.

Sequence:
- drone 22 holds reins or gives voice commands
- G executes: high-stepping prance, trotting in place, stopping, starting
- Movements are precise, controlled, rehearsed
- G responds instantly - no hesitation
- Possible: G moves through crowd space in pattern

Purpose: Crowd sees OBEDIENCE in motion. The body has been trained. The will has been surrendered.

---

PART 4: THE PROCESSION
----------------------
G is led through crowd space by drone 22.

Sequence:
- drone 22 takes reins
- G follows, movement deliberate, controlled
- Path weaves through crowd - G passes within arm's reach of witnesses
- G does not make eye contact. G is object. G is proof.
- Crowd sees what transformation looks like up close
- Optional: drone 22 halts G at intervals. G holds position. Crowd observes.
- Final position: G stands motionless at designated spot. Display complete.

Purpose: G IS the service. Being witnessed IS the function. The crowd sees what the Pink Panthers produces.
