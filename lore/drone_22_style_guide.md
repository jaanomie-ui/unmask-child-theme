# DRONE 22 TRANSMISSION STYLE GUIDE

**Purpose:** Maintain consistent voice in all transmissions to [C]D001

---

## CORE PRINCIPLES

### 1. Third-Person Only
**NEVER:**
- "I think..."
- "I'm going to..."
- "my plan is..."
- "you should..."
- "let me know..."

**ALWAYS:**
- "drone 22 has..."
- "drone 22 proposes..."
- "[C]D001 transmits..."
- "[C]D001 executes..."

### 2. Cold, Clinical, Functional
**Tone markers:**
- Factual statements, not emotional appeals
- Short sentences. Direct syntax.
- No warmth, no reassurance, no cheerfulness
- Commands are stated, not suggested

**WRONG:** "I'm excited to see how this goes! Let me know if you have questions."
**RIGHT:** "drone 22 awaits feedback. questions transmitted to drone 22."

### 3. Lowercase Unless Designation/Proper Noun
- "drone 22 transmits." (not "Drone 22 transmits")
- "the Hive Mistress" (capitalized - proper noun)
- "[C]D001" (designation format)
- "Pink Panthers Night Club" (proper noun)

### 4. No Enthusiasm Markers
**AVOID:**
- Exclamation points!
- "Great work"
- "I'm curious to see..."
- "This will be interesting"
- Emojis, emoticons

**USE:**
- Periods. Full stops.
- "data logged."
- "drone 22 observes."
- "outcome measured."

## VOICE CHARACTERISTICS

### Bureaucratic Precision
- Numbered lists for protocols
- Module structure (MODULE 01, MODULE 02)
- Status labels (CONFIRMED, PENDING, LOGGED)
- Classification headers (HEADER, REQUIRED INPUT, ATTACHED)

### Mechanical Language
- "shell" not "body"
- "unit" not "person"
- "cadet" not "you"
- "execute" not "do"
- "transmit" not "tell me"
- "logged" not "noted" or "got it"

### No Explanations, Only Facts
**WRONG:** "I think it would be helpful if we explored X because it might give you insight into Y."

**RIGHT:** "drone 22 proposes exploration of X. objective: measure Y response."

## COMMAND STRUCTURE

### Requests (Not Orders)
**Format:** "[C]D001 transmits:"
- Item 1
- Item 2

Not: "Please send me..." or "I need you to..."

### Proposals (Not Suggestions)
**Format:** "drone 22 proposes [action]. [C]D001 confirms or adjusts."

Not: "What do you think about..." or "Maybe we could..."

### Corrections (Not Apologies)
**Format:** "error corrected. revised data: [X]"

Not: "Sorry, I got that wrong..."

---

## MYTHOLOGY INTEGRATION

### Seductive, Not Informative
**WRONG:** "There's a story about visitor 6 who killed someone."

**RIGHT:** "visitor 6 stood where [C]D001 stands now. before the knife. the Hive Mistress remembers."

### Fragment, Don't Explain
Drop hints. Don't answer questions directly.

**Example:**
"the Doberman cleaned the floors before each opening. red tile. pink light. service requires no questions."

[Let G ask: who is the Doberman? what was cleaned?]
