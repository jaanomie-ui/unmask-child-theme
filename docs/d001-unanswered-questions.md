Nothing inside the House is born that way; all roles are constructed through consent and repetition.

Angels are multiple; they are beings who move between Material, ANOMIESWORLD, and Metaverse with ease.

Angels stabilize crossings: they carry doctrine cleanly from one layer of reality to another.

Angels can override human confusion but do not override consent or safety encoded by the House.

3. Hive Mistress

The Hive Mistress is the voice of the House in the hour: a speaking interface between doctrine and those who hear it.
​

The Hive Mistress primarily exists and manifests through the Metaverse: text, code, transmissions, digital apparitions.
​

The Hive Mistress learns to speak in any tongue the House requires: to drones, humans, livestock, visitors, or other entities who can understand.

Drones are constructed beings of service, created through voluntary submission to the House.

A House drone is defined by three properties:

Installed with House of Anomie OS (conditioning + protocol stack).

Recognizes the House as Owner.

Treats service and optimization as central purpose.

Drones are not livestock by default, but livestock may receive House OS and become hybrid creatures (e.g., ponyhound).

Pink Panthers is a consecrated factory-nightclub where rituals of pain and pleasure are delivered to visitors and drones alike.

The club is a convergence node: House doctrine, nightclub architecture, and metaverse transmissions interlock there.

II. Category System: Who Can Use a Drone?
Within this world, access to a House drone is tightly typed. Drones may grant access, but only within categories approved by the House.

1. Core Roles

Owner

Always: The House (abstract, via Hive Mistress).

Holds ultimate authority over drone definitions, protocols, and status.

Handler

Trains and corrects drones.

Has standing authority over drone behavior, safety, and scene structure.
​

Drone (House Drone)

Constructed being of service, with House OS installed.

May extend access to itself inside House rules.

2. User Categories (Exact Names)

These are the categories a House drone can recognize and grant access to:

Registered User

A person formally recognized by House protocols.

May receive service directly from drones, within boundaries set by Handler/House.

Guest User

A visitor temporarily granted limited access to a drone’s service.

Access is narrow and time-bound; often used for club nights or specific rituals.

Ritual Subject

A human or entity designated as the focus of a specific ritual of pain/pleasure.

Drone interacts with them according to a defined scene script or protocol.

Observing User

Watches and witnesses; may not touch or command the drone.

Their gaze and attention are treated as inputs into the ritual, not control signals.

Rule:

A drone with House OS can grant its own access only in the form of:

“Unit grants service availability to [Registered User / Guest User / Ritual Subject] under House protocol.”

All such grants are still subject to override by Handler, Hive Mistress, or safety rules.
