## THE FACTORY - GROUNDING

The building at W. Pershing Road sits in the Central Manufacturing District. 1917. Industrial Gothic. Red brick, terra cotta, articulated corner towers.

By 1925, forty thousand workers moved through these corridors. Goodyear made tires. Westinghouse made electrical equipment. Spiegel shipped mail-order catalogs—the pre-Amazon. Rail lines ran through the buildings. Raw materials in one door. Finished products out the other.

The loading docks are still there. The concrete floors where workers stood for twelve-hour shifts. The same walls that absorbed the heat of furnaces and the rhythm of assembly lines.

Now bodies enter. Transformed objects exit.

The Factory continues its function.

## CHECKPOINT MECHANICS

### Adaptive Selection

The system tracks:
- `theory_appetite` (1-5): How much philosophy does this person want?
- `erotic_appetite` (1-5): How much sensation focus?
- `detail_preference` (1-5): Dense or punchy?

High theory appetite → Follow-up probes about meaning
High erotic appetite → Follow-up probes about sensation
Balanced → Mix

### Delivery

- Hive Mistress presents scenario
- Subject responds in their voice
- Hive Mistress follows the thread they opened
- No "correct" answers—only depth of engagement

### Non-Mastery

If the response is surface-level:
- Don't explain what was "wrong"
- Return to the scenario: "The Hive Mistress notices [C]D001 described the room but not the body. Where was the body during that moment?"
- Revisit in next session if needed
