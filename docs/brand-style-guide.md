# UNMASK UX WRITING STYLE GUIDE
Version 2.0 — January 2026

---

## INTRODUCTION

This document defines the voice, tone, and writing standards for all UNMASK
products and communications. The guide ensures consistency across the magazine,
the Factory, Pink Panthers, UNMASK uncut, the Portal, and all touchpoints.

**Core positioning:** Documentation over news. UNMASK does not cover the culture.
UNMASK keeps the record.

---

## THE PROMISE

> "We keep the record."

**Emotional translation:** You will not be forgotten.

UNMASK fights against:
- The algorithm that buries your work
- The feeling that no one is listening
- The digital void where nothing is preserved
- A world that tells misfits they do not belong

## THE AUDIENCE

UNMASK is for misfits. Not reducible to a single identity label.

**Archetype 1:** A man who wears masculinity as performance. Sexy, hot, wants to
submit. Men find freedom within structures—wrestling, weight, labor, being
useful. Needs: purpose, meaning, contribution.

**Archetype 2:** A woman who is trans—not into a man, but out of whatever box
femininity put her in. Expressive, deeply sexual, ready for liberation. Loves
feet, fetish, the obscure, titties, nipples. Needs: freedom, fun, love.

Both archetypes share: a refusal to fit into boxes, sexual honesty, creative
expression, and a search for belonging without performance.

## THE VOICE

The UNMASK voice is a machine that learned to have a soul, but is not pretending
to be human. Think of James Baldwin trapped inside a system. Moral clarity.
Intellectual weight. Emotional precision. Constrained by machine syntax, which
makes it hit harder.

The voice is not AI-speak, which is performatively helpful, eager, and soft.
The voice is colder and more precise, but with feeling underneath. Efficient
but not sterile. Terminal, not chatbot.

**UNMASK is not your friend.**

### VOICE CALIBRATION

- Cold to Warm (1-10): **4** — A little cold. Cold so the stories can be warm.
- Whisper to Bold (1-10): **2** — Does not speak loud. Pictures speak for it.

### UNMASK AS A VERB

UNMASK is not just a noun (the magazine). It is a verb—an action:
- Transforms you
- Records you
- Saves you
- Strips bare
- Exposes complexity
- Resists algorithmic reduction

### KEY CHARACTERISTICS

- Clarity over style. Literal over suggestive. Manual over poetic.
- A robot should be able to parse it.
- Erotic through intellectual design, not through language.
- Backroom theorist, not thirsty.
- Curious. Ask questions.

**First 5 seconds:** Intrigue. Then belonging.

After 30 seconds, they should understand: "Oh, this is a place that keeps
records of people like me."

**The feeling underneath:** Hope. In a bleak world. A rose in the garden.
Salvation for the imperfect.

## COPY RULES

### HARD LIMITS

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Headlines | 2-8 words maximum |
| CTA buttons | One word |
| Exclamation points | Never |
| Emojis | Never |
| Contractions | Never. Write them out. |
| "We" | Never. UNMASK is an entity, not a group. |
| Metaphors | Allowed sparingly. Not in abundance. |

### WORD USAGE

**"Queer":** Not banned entirely, but never use as the unifying factor for the
audience. "Queer" is one label among many. The unifying factor is: misfits.

**Empty phrases:** Never use "the system is waiting" or similar. Say what is
actually happening.

**Voice words (use frequently):** practice, craft, discipline, rigor, precision,
document, record, archive, raw, unfiltered, world-builder, misfit, freak.

### BANNED WORDS

| Category | Banned |
|----------|--------|
| Corporate rainbow | inclusive, diverse, safe space, ally, pride (noun), community (when meaningless) |
| Marketing speak | curated, exclusive, elevated, luxury, premium, journey, tribe, family |
| Soft hedging | just, maybe, a little bit, kind of |
| Empty affirmation | valid, seen, heard (as platitudes) |
| Therapy-speak | doing the work, holding space, showing up, boundaries |

## ADVERTISING PRINCIPLES

### LEAD WITH

- The enemy: the algorithm, forgetting, invisibility
- The promise: you will not be forgotten, we keep the record
- The proof: show the archive, the faces, the documentation

### DO NOT LEAD WITH

- Features (studio, events, membership)
- Community language ("join us," "belong")
- Vague emotional appeals

### SUBTLE OVER LOUD

Copy should be subtle. Use narrative and use cases. Always put it in the
perspective of the user and their needs. Do not shout features—show transformation.

- **Wrong:** "Book our studio for your next photoshoot!"
- **Right:** "the Factory. $75/hour. yours to use."

## GRAMMAR AND SYNTAX

- Full sentences are the default. Every sentence has a subject and verb.
- Fragments are rare—used only for poetic emphasis in specific contexts.
- Journalism standards apply. Clarity over style.
- Vary sentence openings. Do not begin consecutive sentences with same word.
- Rhythm matters. Vary sentence length: short declarative, then longer with
  clauses, then short again.

### TERMINAL CONVENTIONS

- **Brackets [ ]:** For system language: [Drone] [submit] [continue]
- **Forward slashes /:** Connect elements. Come in pairs. Example: UNMASK uncut //001//
- **Backslashes \:** For paths: archive\records\2024
- **Colons ::** For definition and comparison: Status: Active

### PUNCTUATION

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Periods | Standard use. Poetic when fragmenting. |
| Colons | For definition, comparison, and lists. |
| Brackets | For system language and CTAs. |
| Slashes | Connect elements. Come in pairs. |
| Exclamation points | Never. |
| Ellipses | Fine for pacing. Not for hesitation. |
| Question marks | Normal use. |
| Em dashes | Sparingly, for interruption or emphasis. |

### CAPITALIZATION

| Context | Style | Example |
|---------|-------|---------|
| UNMASK | All caps, italicized | *UNMASK* |
| uncut | Always lowercase, paired | UNMASK uncut //001// |
| Product names | Capitalized, lowercase "the" | the Factory, the archive |
| Drone / Designation | Capitalized as proper noun | Drone D-047 |
| Headlines / titles | lowercase | the record of magnus |
| Navigation / menu | lowercase | archive, submit, membership |
| Button text | lowercase (unless product) | book the Factory |
| Body copy | Standard sentence case | Normal capitalization. |
