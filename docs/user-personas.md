# UNMASK USER PERSONAS
Version 1.0 — January 2026

---

## OVERVIEW

UNMASK serves misfits — creative individuals aged 21-40 in urban areas who seek
belonging, inclusion, and transformation through art. They refuse to fit into
boxes, embrace sexual honesty and creative expression, and search for belonging
without performance.

Six primary personas move through the UNMASK ecosystem. Each has distinct needs,
entry points, and conversion paths.

---

## PERSONA 1: THE CURIOUS

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age Range | 21-26 |
| Primary Need | Safe exploration of identity |
| Primary Product | Identity System (Portal, Dossier) |
| Color Code | #e91e63 (Pink) |

### PROFILE

Young, still forming identity. Has questions about who they are and who they
could become. Not yet committed to any scene or community. Exploring.

### WHAT THEY WANT

- A space to try on identities without judgment
- To see others like them documented
- Permission to be uncertain
- Low-stakes entry point

### WHAT BRINGS THEM IN

- Instagram discovery (striking imagery)
- Friend shares a profile/record
- Searching for representation
- Curiosity about subcultures

### FRICTION POINTS

- Lore-heavy language feels intimidating
- Not sure if they "belong" here
- Fear of being seen before they are ready
- Membership feels like commitment

### CONVERSION PATH

Stranger → Browse profiles → Create account → Complete dossier →
Post first ISO → Receive response → Realize they belong

### KEY METRIC

Account creation rate: Target 5% of visitors

| Age Range | 33-40 |
| Primary Need | Kink-aware community, disposable income |
| Primary Product | UNMASK uncut / Drone Membership |

### PROFILE

Established professional. Has money but limited time. Wants a space where kink
is normalized, not sensationalized. Values discretion. Looking for depth, not
breadth. Wants to support something they believe in.

### WHAT THEY WANT

- Community that understands kink without explaining it
- High-quality content delivered to them
- To support independent media
- Escape from corporate/vanilla world

### WHAT BRINGS THEM IN

- Word of mouth in kink communities
- Sees Magazine content that respects kink
- Looking for subscription-based support model
- Tired of algorithm-driven platforms

### FRICTION POINTS

- Privacy concerns (using real identity)

### CONVERSION PATH

Discover Magazine → Browse content → Value the mission →
Subscribe as Drone → Receive UNMASK uncut → Attend Pink Panthers →
Book Factory for personal use

Content browser → Drone conversion rate: Target 2%

## SHARED CHARACTERISTICS

All UNMASK personas share:
- Refusal to fit into boxes
- Sexual honesty (varying degrees of openness)
- Creative expression as identity
- Search for belonging without performance
- Distrust of algorithm-driven platforms
- Value documentation over content
- Appreciate craft and rigor

## ARCHETYPE ANCHORS

Beyond the six functional personas, two archetypal anchors represent the
emotional core of who UNMASK serves:

### ANCHOR 1: THE MASCULINE SUBMISSIVE

A man who wears masculinity as performance. Sexy, hot, wants to submit. Men
find freedom within structures—wrestling, weight, labor, being useful.

**Needs:** purpose, meaning, contribution.

### ANCHOR 2: THE TRANS FEMININE

A woman who is trans—not into a man, but out of whatever box femininity put
her in. Expressive, deeply sexual, ready for liberation. Loves feet, fetish,
the obscure, titties, nipples.

**Needs:** freedom, fun, love.

These anchors inform tone and imagery across all products.
