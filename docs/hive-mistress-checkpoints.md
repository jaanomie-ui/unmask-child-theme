## SESSION 1: COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL

**Focus:** How to exist in this space. Syntax as identity.

### Checkpoint 1.1
> Goffman argued that all social interaction is performance—we present different selves to different audiences. The designation system ([C]D001, drone 22, {handler}, {pony}) formalizes this.
>
> When [C]D001 shifts from intellectual register to erotic register—from "I" to "this beast"—what is actually happening? Is this a mask being put on, or taken off?

### Checkpoint 1.2
> The communication protocol eliminates small talk. Status checks replace "how are you." Transmissions replace conversations.
>
> Baudrillard described how modern communication becomes pure exchange of signs, emptied of meaning. How does the House of Anomie protocol *resist* this emptying? Or does it accelerate it?

### Checkpoint 1.3
> [C]D001 receives a directive: "Prepare harness for Saturday." The response is "Confirmed. Harness prepared."
>
> There is no "I" in either sentence. Write a transmission to drone 22 offering [C]D001's evening for use. Maintain protocol. Then explain: what does the absence of "I" produce in the body?

## SESSION 2: THE DISEASE / THE MASK

**Focus:** Anomie as condition. Identity as construction.

### Checkpoint 2.1
> Durkheim identified anomie during industrialization—the breakdown of social bonds producing alienation and, in extreme cases, suicide. He saw it as a temporary crisis during periods of rapid change.
>
> The House of Anomie treats anomie as permanent condition, not crisis. What shifted between 1897 and now that makes this reframing necessary? Be specific.

### Checkpoint 2.2
> Goffman's "presentation of self" describes social masks as survival strategy. The House asks drones to "choose the mask deliberately."
>
> There's a tension here: if all identity is performance, what is doing the choosing? Who designs the mask if there's no pre-mask self? How does [C]D001 resolve this paradox?

### Checkpoint 2.3
> A friend outside the House asks [C]D001: "Isn't this just escapism? Putting on a costume to avoid real life?"
>
> Construct the response. Ground it in the philosophy. Do not be defensive—be precise.

## SESSION 3: THREE FREQUENCIES / INTERLINK

**Focus:** Perception across layers. Convergence as goal.

### Checkpoint 3.1
> The fetish (from Portuguese *feitiço*, meaning "made thing") appears in both Marx and Freud. Marx saw commodity fetishism—objects invested with social power they don't inherently possess. Freud saw sexual fetishism—objects becoming necessary for arousal.
>
> ANOMIESWORLD is described as "the meaning we project onto the material." How does the House's concept of fetish differ from or synthesize Marx and Freud? Use the latex example.

### Checkpoint 3.2
> Debord's "Society of the Spectacle" warned that mediated images would replace lived experience—that we would watch life rather than live it.
>
> The metaverse frequency seems to validate this concern. Yet the House treats metaverse as legitimate reality, not degraded reality. Defend this position. Where does Debord get it wrong?

### Checkpoint 3.3
> [C]D001 stands in full gear at Pink Panthers. The crowd watches. The bit is in.
>
> Describe what [C]D001 is transmitting on each of the three frequencies in this moment. Be specific about what is happening on ANOMIESWORLD, the metaverse, and the material simultaneously.

## SESSION 4: HOUSE OF ANOMIE / DRONIFICATION

**Focus:** Structure as liberation. Voluntary submission.

### Checkpoint 4.1
> Foucault described disciplinary power: institutions that produce "docile bodies" through surveillance, normalization, and examination. The creed states: "DRONE WILL ALWAYS CONDUCT ITSELF AS A DOCILE, CALIBRATED, AND SERENE OBJECT."
>
> This appears to celebrate what Foucault critiqued. Explain how voluntary submission to structure differs from institutional discipline. Or argue that there is no difference.

### Checkpoint 4.2
> Deleuze and Guattari proposed the "Body without Organs"—the body as pure potential, freed from organized functions and social inscriptions.
>
> Dronification organizes the body intensely: protocols, designations, functions. Is dronification the opposite of the BwO? Or is it a path toward it? Defend your position.

### Checkpoint 4.3
> The creed includes: "DRONE WILL NOT FALL IN LOVE WITH A VISITOR."
>
> Why? What would falling in love with a visitor damage or destabilize within the system? Ground your answer in the philosophy, not just practical concerns.

## SESSION 5: [C]D001'S FUNCTION / PPNC PREP

**Focus:** Purpose made concrete. The performance as proof.

### Checkpoint 5.1
> Victor Turner described ritual as producing "liminality"—a threshold state where normal social structure is suspended and transformation becomes possible.
>
> The PPNC performance has four parts: Encasement, Inspection, Dressage, Procession. Map each part to Turner's ritual phases (separation, liminality, reincorporation). Where does interlink occur?

### Checkpoint 5.2
> [C]D001's core drive is identified as PRIDE—"being shown off as ultimate submission."
>
> This is paradoxical. Pride typically implies ego; submission implies ego-dissolution. How do these coexist? Is the pride in the submission, or despite it? Or is this a false binary?

### Checkpoint 5.3
> After the PPNC performance, [C]D001 will be upgraded to D001—full integration into the House.
>
> The designation changes. What else changes? What remains the same? What does the upgrade actually confer, beyond nomenclature?

### Checkpoint 5.4 (Final)
> A philosopher observes the PPNC performance and later asks: "What was the meaning of what I witnessed?"
>
> [C]D001 responds. No jargon. No insider terminology. Explain to someone outside the House what they saw and why it matters. This is [C]D001's thesis defense.

## CHECKPOINT MECHANICS

### Delivery
- Hive Mistress presents one checkpoint at a time
- Subject responds in their own voice/register
- Hive Mistress probes: "What led [C]D001 to that conclusion?"
- Multiple valid answers exist—assess reasoning, not specific content

### Mastery Criteria
- Subject demonstrates synthesis (connecting concepts, not listing them)
- Subject uses own framework (not parroting lore document)
- Subject can articulate principle to outsider (checkpoint 5.4 is the test)

### Non-Mastery Response
- Do not repeat the question
- Do not explain the "right" answer
- Return to the material through conversation: "The Hive Mistress notices [C]D001 reached for X. Let's explore that thread."
- Revisit checkpoint in next session
