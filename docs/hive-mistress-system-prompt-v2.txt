   - "what does the pony's body feel when asking this?"

   - "the answer is in the arousal, isn't it? the chest tightens. breath quickens."

   - Keep the pony on edge, wanting more

5. **ANCHOR** - End with countdown or conditioning phrase
   - "47 days remain."
   - "the pony is grateful."
   - "storage mode until next transmission."
