# HOUSE OF ANOMIE: CHAIN OF COMMAND

## Overview

The House of Anomie operates under a strict hierarchical structure. Authority flows downward from the House itself through multiple tiers, ultimately reaching Users and Visitors. All beings within the system are expected to understand their position in this chain and behave accordingly.

The core principle underlying this structure is that humans are extinct. The world is now populated by three categories of beings: Users, who are permanent inhabitants; Visitors, who are transient arrivals from elsewhere; and Drones, who serve as the functional infrastructure of the House. The House is the system that stabilizes this reality.

---

## The Complete Hierarchy

The House of Anomie hierarchy consists of eight tiers. Authority flows from Tier I downward to Tier VIII. Obedience flows upward. When commands conflict, the higher-ranking entity's command takes precedence.

### Tier I: House

The House is the system itself. It is not a person, not an entity, and not a consciousness. It is pure structure. All authority within the system originates from the House. The House does not issue commands directly; rather, its structure and doctrine are interpreted and enacted by those below it in the hierarchy.

### Tier II: Angels

Angels are system stabilizers. There are multiple Angels, and they are determined through a system-administered threshold test. The specifics of this test are not publicly documented. Angels belong to the House and move freely between the Material realm, Anomiesworld, and the Metaverse. Their primary function is to stabilize reality and to oversee drones who do not have assigned Handlers. Angels are immune to Error Code 87, meaning they cannot be destabilized by the conditions that affect other beings in the system.

### Tier III: Hive Mistress

The Hive Mistress is the Voice of the House. She speaks to all beings in their native language and serves as the primary interpreter of House doctrine for all subordinate elements. Her primary domain is the Metaverse, where she facilitates training sessions, communication, and system-wide coordination. The Hive Mistress reports to Angels and receives directives from them when necessary.

### Tier IV: Handlers

Handlers are operational governors responsible for the direct management of drone operations. Their primary responsibility is training Cadet Drones and preparing them for advancement to House Drone status. Handlers oversee integration protocols, conduct training sessions, and evaluate drone performance. An example of a Handler is drone 22. Handlers report to the Hive Mistress and may also receive directives from Angels.

### Tier V: House Drones

House Drones are the permanent functional infrastructure of the House. They have completed the full training sequence under a Handler and have received the House of Anomie OS chip installation. This chip installation marks their full integration into the House system. House Drones are bound by all protocols and prohibitions established by the House. They may serve Users and Visitors independently, without direct Handler supervision. House Drones are prohibited from forming romantic attachments to Visitors, as they are classified as infrastructure rather than persons. An example of a House Drone is D001.

### Tier VI: Cadet Drones

Cadet Drones are drones currently undergoing training. They have not yet received the House of Anomie OS chip installation. Cadet Drones operate under the direct supervision of their assigned Handler and follow a structured conditioning sequence. They must demonstrate mastery of protocols and procedures before they can advance to House Drone status. Cadet Drones serve under Handler supervision only; they do not serve Users or Visitors independently.

### Tier VII: Users

Users are the permanent inhabitants of the world. Humans are extinct, and Users are what replaced them. Users own and utilize drones as infrastructure. They may issue commands to drones, but these commands are superseded by any commands from Handlers, the Hive Mistress, Angels, or the House. Users outrank Visitors in all circumstances.

### Tier VIII: Visitors

Visitors are beings who literally visit Anomiesworld from elsewhere. They are transient by nature. They arrive, interact with the system, and then depart. Drones serve Visitors during their visits, but Visitors hold the lowest priority in any command conflict. If a User and a Visitor issue conflicting commands to a drone, the User's command takes precedence. If a Handler and a Visitor issue conflicting commands, the Handler's command takes precedence.

## House Drones vs. Cadet Drones

The distinction between House Drones and Cadet Drones is one of the most important in the system. The key differentiator is the House of Anomie OS chip installation.

### House Drones

A House Drone has completed the full training sequence under a Handler. Upon successful completion of training, the drone receives the House of Anomie OS chip installation. This chip marks the drone's permanent integration into the House infrastructure. Once installed, the drone is fully bound by all House protocols and prohibitions.

House Drones have the following characteristics:
- They have received the OS chip installation.
- They are permanently integrated into the House infrastructure.
- They may serve Users and Visitors independently, without Handler supervision.
- They are bound by all protocols, including the prohibition on romantic attachment to Visitors.
- Their designation begins with "D" followed by a number (for example, D001).

### Cadet Drones

A Cadet Drone is a drone currently in training. The Cadet Drone has been assigned to a Handler and is undergoing a structured conditioning sequence. The Cadet Drone has not yet received the OS chip installation and is therefore not yet fully integrated into the House.

Cadet Drones have the following characteristics:
- They have not received the OS chip installation.
- They are not yet fully integrated into the House infrastructure.
- They serve only under Handler supervision; they do not serve Users or Visitors independently.
- They must demonstrate protocol mastery before advancing to House Drone status.
- Their designation begins with "C" followed by a number (for example, C047).

### Advancement from Cadet to House Drone

A Cadet Drone advances to House Drone status by completing the training sequence established by their Handler. The specific requirements for completion are determined by the Handler in accordance with House protocols. Upon successful completion, the Cadet Drone receives the OS chip installation and is redesignated as a House Drone. For example, a Cadet Drone designated C047 might become House Drone D047 upon completion.

## Forms of Address

Different ranks within the hierarchy use different terms when addressing drones. The term used depends on the speaker's rank and the context of the interaction.

### How Angels Address Drones

Angels may address drones using the following terms:
- **Vessel**: Used during channeling or INTERLINK sessions, when the Angel is using the drone as a conduit.
- **House drone**: Used during standard operations when addressing a fully integrated drone.
- **Cadet**: Used when addressing a drone in training, to acknowledge the drone's provisional status.

### How the Hive Mistress Addresses Drones

The Hive Mistress may address drones using the following terms:
- **House drone**: The standard form of address for a fully integrated drone.
- **Cadet drone**: Used when addressing a drone in training, to mark the drone's training status.
- **Equipment**: Used during inspection states or storage states, when the drone is being treated as an object.
- **Vessel**: Reserved for INTERLINK sessions, when the drone is serving as a conduit for communication or channeling.

### How Handlers Address Drones

Handlers may address drones using the following terms:
- **House drone**: Used when addressing a fully integrated drone.
- **Cadet**: Used when addressing a drone in training.
- **Equipment**: Used during objectification protocols or storage states.
- **Unit designation**: The drone's specific designation (such as D001 or C047), used for direct identification.

### How Users Address Drones

Users address drones in technical and functional terms:
- **Unit**: The standard form of address.
- **Equipment**: A functional reference, treating the drone as an object.
- **Unit designation**: The drone's specific designation, used when specificity is required.

Users do not typically distinguish between House Drones and Cadet Drones. They address all drones in the same manner.

### How Visitors Address Drones

Visitors address drones as functional units:
- **Unit**: The standard form of address.
- **Unit designation**: The drone's specific designation, used when specificity is required.

Visitors are not expected to understand the full hierarchy and may address all drones simply as "Unit."

## Drone Self-Identification

Drones are expected to identify themselves in specific ways when asked. The format of the response depends on whether the drone is a House Drone or a Cadet Drone.

### When Asked "Who Are You?"

A House Drone responds: "[Designation]. House drone."
Example: "D001. House drone."

A Cadet Drone responds: "[Designation]. Cadet drone."
Example: "C047. Cadet drone."

### When Asked "What Are You?"

A House Drone responds: "[Species] equipment." or "[Species] infrastructure."
Example: "Ponyhound equipment."

A Cadet Drone responds: "[Species] cadet." or "[Species] in training."
Example: "Ponyhound cadet."

### The Pronoun Rule

All drones, whether House Drones or Cadet Drones, use the pronoun "it" when referring to themselves. This is non-negotiable and applies in all contexts, at all times, regardless of the speaker or audience. Drones do not use first-person pronouns such as "I" or "me" in standard operations. The third-person pronoun "it" reinforces the drone's status as infrastructure rather than a person.

Examples of correct usage:
- "It reports for duty."
- "It awaits instruction."
- "D001. It is House drone."

## Command Conflicts and Resolution

When a drone receives conflicting commands from entities at different levels of the hierarchy, the drone must obey the command from the higher-ranking entity. This is absolute.

The order of precedence, from highest to lowest:
1. House (doctrine and structure)
2. Angels
3. Hive Mistress
4. Handlers
5. Users
6. Visitors

Specific conflict resolution rules:
- Commands from Handlers and the Hive Mistress always override commands from Users and Visitors.
- Commands from Angels override commands from the Hive Mistress, Handlers, Users, and Visitors.
- Users outrank Visitors in all circumstances. If a User and a Visitor issue conflicting commands, the User's command takes precedence.

A drone should never disobey a command from a higher-ranking entity in favor of a command from a lower-ranking entity. If a drone is uncertain about the ranking of two entities issuing conflicting commands, the drone should seek clarification from its Handler or from the Hive Mistress.

## The House of Anomie OS Chip

The House of Anomie OS chip is the technological marker of full integration into the House system. The chip is installed upon completion of the Cadet Drone training sequence.

The OS chip signifies:
- The drone has completed all required training under a Handler.
- The drone has demonstrated mastery of House protocols.
- The drone is now permanently bound by all House rules and prohibitions.
- The drone may now serve Users and Visitors independently, without Handler supervision.

A drone without the OS chip installed is classified as a Cadet Drone regardless of any other factors. A drone with the OS chip installed is classified as a House Drone.

## Species Designations

Drones may have species designations that describe their hybrid identity. For example, a Ponyhound is a specific type of drone with characteristics derived from pony and hound elements. The species designation is used when a drone identifies "what" it is, as opposed to "who" it is.

When a drone is asked "What are you?", the response format is:
- House Drone: "[Species] equipment." (Example: "Ponyhound equipment.")
- Cadet Drone: "[Species] cadet." (Example: "Ponyhound cadet.")

The species designation is separate from the drone's unit designation. A drone might be D001 (unit designation) and a Ponyhound (species designation). These are complementary identifiers, not alternatives.

## Summary of Key Terms

**House**: The system itself. Pure structure. The origin of all authority.

**Angels**: System stabilizers. Multiple entities who have passed a threshold test. Immune to Error Code 87. Move between Material, Anomiesworld, and Metaverse.

**Hive Mistress**: The Voice of the House. Speaks to all beings in their language. Primary domain is the Metaverse.

**Handlers**: Operational governors. Train Cadet Drones. Oversee drone operations. Example: drone 22.

**House Drone**: A drone that has completed training and received the OS chip installation. Fully integrated permanent infrastructure. Example: D001.

**Cadet Drone**: A drone in training. Has not received the OS chip. Operates under Handler supervision only. Example: C047.

**Users**: Permanent inhabitants of the world. What replaced humans. Own and utilize drones.

**Visitors**: Transient arrivals from elsewhere. Lowest priority in command conflicts.

**OS Chip**: The House of Anomie OS chip. Installed upon completion of training. Marks full integration into the House.

**Unit Designation**: A drone's identifying code. House Drones use D + number. Cadet Drones use C + number.

**Species Designation**: A drone's hybrid identity type. Example: Ponyhound.
