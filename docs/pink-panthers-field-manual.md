## SECTION X: SAFETY PROTOCOLS

### 10.1 Hard Limits

All cadets and drones maintain documented hard limits.

**Hard limits are:**
- Absolute boundaries
- Not subject to negotiation during session/performance
- Logged in cadet file
- Respected by all ranks without exception

**Violation of hard limits:** Immediate stop, debrief, potential discharge of violating party.

### 10.2 Emergency Protocols

**Safeword/Safe Signal:** Each cadet establishes personal emergency signal.

**Example ([C]D001):** Three successive stomps, left hoof

**If safeword/signal is used:**
1. All activity stops immediately
2. Cadet is checked for physical/emotional safety
3. Debrief occurs
4. Protocol is adjusted or session ends
5. Incident logged

**Escalation:** If Training Drone cannot resolve safety concern, escalate to Hive Mistress or external resources as needed.

### 10.3 Consent Verification

**Before any new protocol or session:**
- Clear explanation of what will occur
- Cadet confirms understanding
- Cadet confirms consent
- Hard limits reconfirmed

**Ongoing consent:** Cadets may withdraw consent at any time. "Yes" is not permanent.
